# Provenance and external dependencies

- The mask geometry, named components, preview materials and project-specific export/build scripts were created for this project. No downloaded mask model, logo, texture pack or third-party source package is included.
- The `.blend` was reconstructed in a fresh scene using only those mask meshes and the single-Root attachment rig. No character head, original glasses mesh, game texture, linked library or packed image is included.
- The rig's attachment transform and the symbolic game asset/parameter names are retained for compatibility with the game's glasses accessory slot. Their presence does not include or grant rights to the game's assets.
- Blender, Unreal Engine, Stellar Blade and CNS are separate dependencies and are not bundled. Obtain them from their respective providers and follow their applicable terms. This repository is not affiliated with their creators.
- The [Stellar Blade Modding Guide](https://github.com/Stellar-Blade-Modding-Team/Stellar-Blade-Modding-Guide/wiki/Materials) was consulted for the material workflow. No guide text or third-party implementation is vendored here.

- Manufacturer and patent references informed proportions and construction ideas: [Dräger X-plore 5500](https://www.draeger.com/Content/Documents/Products/x-plore-5500-pi-DMC-113276-en-us.pdf) for compact lateral filters and a separate nose cup; [Avon FM54](https://www.avon-protection.com/media/ue1iluwy/avon-protection_fm54-respirator_product-brochure_en.pdf) for a panoramic visor, twin ports and silicone nosecups; and [Avon patent WO2024074487A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024074487A1/en) for the distinction between a breathing cavity and a conforming chin seal. These are design-inspiration references, not sources of copied mesh, texture, logo or CAD assets. The side hardware and larger cup were modeled for this project.
- Earlier full-face-mask references included [Ocean Reef](https://diving.oceanreefgroup.com/full-face-masks/) and [Interspiro Divator](https://interspiro.com/en-gb/products/divator-full-face-mask?VariantID=VO62.VO64.VO36). No manufacturer affiliation or endorsement is implied.

- Inner-cup inlet-valve placement and shallow disc/seat details were informed by the [3M 6000 Series instructions, Nose Cup Assembly 6894 and Inhalation Valve 6893, pp. 17–18](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/95995O/3m-full-facepiece-respirator-6000-series-user-instructions.pdf#page=17) and [Dräger X-plore 5500/6000 technical manual, section 3.11, p. 10](https://www.draeger.com/Content/Documents/Products/xplore-ifu-9300783-de-me.pdf#page=10). The project models its own paired valve details on the enlarged cup; no manufacturer part model or image is bundled.
